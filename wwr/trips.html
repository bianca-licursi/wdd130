Our Rafting Adventures

River Explorer

Discover the excitement of rafting while enjoying the natural scenery around the river. This sample experience is designed for visitors who want to enjoy the outdoors, learn basic rafting techniques, and share an adventure with friends or family.

Suggested duration: Half day

Difficulty: Beginner-friendly, subject to river conditions

Rapids Adventure

Experience a more energetic river adventure and discover the importance of communication and teamwork. Participants should follow the guide's instructions and meet the trip's age, health, and safety requirements.

Suggested duration: Full day

Difficulty: Intermediate, depending on conditions

Mountain River Experience

Make a day of outdoor adventure while appreciating the mountain landscape and the beauty of Mendoza. This sample trip focuses on enjoying nature, working together, and creating memorable experiences on the water.

Suggested duration: Full day

Difficulty: Depends on the selected route and river conditions
